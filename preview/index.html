Grammar

Every screen is an instrument: a field you look at, a panel of values you set, and a status line that tells the truth. Eight rules produce everything below.

  1. Line, not fillEvery box is a 1px hairline. No shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners. Depth comes from rules, not from elevation.
  2. Selection is inversionA chosen option becomes an ink block with paper text. Never a tint, never a colored border.
  3. One signalEach product picks one accent. It marks only what is live: pointer, playhead, a modulated value, an engaged mode, focus.
  4. The value is the heroNumbers are huge, light and tabular. The label above is tiny. The unit is tinier.
  5. Labels speak in capsMono, uppercase, tracked. Short nouns: REACH, FLOW, MIX. Separators are spaced · and /.
  6. Say what it meansUnder a ruler, a caption translates the number: STEADY, WARM, EACH NOTE A LITTLE.
  7. Hands on the numbersDrag a value up or down. Shift for fine. Double-click resets. The footer says so, in one line.
  8. Status tells the truthVersion left, metrics right, always visible. Status is a word (OK, WARN), not a traffic light.
